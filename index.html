Research & Projects

NaijaFoodWatch: Food Insecurity Forecasting

MSc thesis · live app

An interpretable, multi-source machine learning framework that forecasts food insecurity 3 and 6 months ahead for Nigerian states. It combines conflict (ACLED), rainfall (CHIRPS) and vegetation (MODIS) data in an averaged ensemble of logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost and LSTM (weighted kappa 0.83). I tested it honestly with a spatial holdout: it ties a persistence baseline overall and weakens in data-scarce southern states, and SHAP shows which drivers matter in each region.

Interests & Vision

My goal is to build AI that people can trust: models that are honest about where they work and where they fail, and that explain their outputs to the people who act on them. I want to apply this to agriculture, food security and education in Africa, and to teach others to do the same, from university classrooms to my evening classes for complete beginners.

Beyond my academic and professional life, I keep a healthy balance through tennis and crossfit, and I enjoy photography and cinema, which sharpen my eye for visual storytelling and design.
